# Asset and licensing note

This repository skeleton introduces no third-party runtime dependencies, web fonts, trackers, or remotely hosted visual assets. Its HTML, CSS, inline signal motif, and deployment workflow are original project scaffolding.

The final presentation and downloadable archives are separate scholarly artifacts. Their claims, charts, photographs, logos, and citations must retain the provenance recorded in the Marp speaker notes and source bundle. In particular:

- Quantitative results are derived from the supplied KDD MILETS manuscript and its figure files.
- Dataset traces and photographs remain governed by their original dataset or media licenses.
- Conference, institutional, and laboratory marks remain the property of their respective organizations.
- Reusable SVG packages should include their accompanying chart/data provenance file.

No license is asserted here over the supplied paper, source deck, external media, institutional marks, or third-party datasets. Add a repository-level software license only after the project owners select the intended terms.
