Central argument

A time-series transformation may change the event—not merely the view.

Flipping or cropping an image usually preserves object identity. Reversal, jitter, permutation, or masking can instead alter temporal order, event morphology, periodic structure, or a clinically relevant transient.

The proposed objective asks an encoder to describe the signal window through properties already grounded in time-series analysis.

Scope of the claim

The evidence covers two wearable-sensing benchmarks and short fixed-length windows. Descriptor-set selection remains consequential; the study does not establish universal superiority across all time-series domains.
